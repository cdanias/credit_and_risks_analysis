Forensic mechanism

estimate

A data-centre loan is only as good as the lease behind it. When a hyperscaler declines to renew, the developer's cash flow disappears before construction debt has amortised, and lenders inherit an asset whose value depends on finding a new tenant and securing power. Google buying a distressed site shows the asset can be rescued, but at a price set by the buyer. The healthy structure is the one IREN now demands: customers prepay 90% prepay of chip costs, so the tenant, not the lender, carries the construction risk.

Forensic mechanism

estimate

A PIK preferred pays its coupon in more paper, so the lender books income without receiving cash. While the borrower grows, the position looks healthy; once growth stalls, the accrued paper is written off and the income stops at once. That is why rising PIK shares (11.98% across BDCs) tend to come before non-accrual jumps like OTF's move from 0.6% to 1.8% cost.
