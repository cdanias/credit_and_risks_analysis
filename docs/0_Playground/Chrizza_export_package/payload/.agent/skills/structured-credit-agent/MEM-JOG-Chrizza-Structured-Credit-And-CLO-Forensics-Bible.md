## 3. Operational Heuristics (Seeded Rules of Thumb)

1. **The Headline Default Myth:** Always compute the Holistic Economic Default Rate ($\text{Defaults}_{\text{Legal}} + \text{LMEs}_{\text{Priming}}$). An out-of-court uptiering exchange that strips non-participating lenders is a default in economic reality.
2. **The CCC Conveyor Belt:** When single-name loans trade below 80 cents, model the 7.5%–10% CCC bucket limit in CLO indentures. Once breached, the excess par is marked at market value, eroding the Junior OC cushion.
3. **The Unrestricted Subsidiary Trap:** Always search credit agreements for J.Crew / Envision style carve-outs where intellectual property or crown-jewel assets can be transferred to an unrestricted subsidiary and used as collateral for new super-priority debt.
4. **Trustee Cut-Off Timestamps:** Never rely on summary news for CLO distributions. Inspect raw trustee monthly payment reports (Intex, BNY Mellon, Alter Domus) stamped with exact point-in-time cut-off dates.
5. **Consensual Wipe-Out as Equity Token:** When junior debt in a whole-business securitisation (e.g. Thames Water Class B) signs a consensual TSA, a nominal equity slice (~5.9%) is merely a legal peace treaty to avoid cramdown litigation; economically, principal recovery is zero.
6. **Multi-Fund Steering Lawsuit as Catalyst:** When fierce competitor funds (Elliott, Arini, Sona, Diameter, King Street) unify to sue a sponsor in NY Commercial Division, drop-down and asset transfer optionality is legally frozen, but recovery transitions from credit risk to legal docket risk. Hard VETO is mandatory.
7. **The Post-RP Deleveraging Windfall vs Junior OC Squeeze (2026-10-28):** Sequential AAA amortisation after a CLO exits its reinvestment period creates a massive Senior OC windfall (>68.0% average in Fitch US BSL surveillance vs 9.3% for reinvesting CLOs), making post-RP AAA/AA notes fortress-safe. Simultaneously, the unamortised collateral pool concentrates into slower-prepaying B3/CCC borrowers, compressing average post-RP Junior OC cushions to 2.1% (210 bps) vs 4.5% (450 bps) in reinvesting deals. Never conflate post-RP senior safety with post-RP BB/B mezzanine safety.
8. **Sister-JV Telecom Contagion via Pod De-Grossing (2026-10-28):** When a mega-cap European telecom obligor suffers a rating downgrade or M&A block (e.g. Virgin Media O2 cut to B+ by Fitch at >6.1x leverage amid UK CMA review of nexfibre/Netomnia), multi-strategy and credit hedge funds immediately de-gross liquid sister-JV paper (driving VodafoneZiggo's €1.125bn 2033 TLB down 6.7 pts to 86.0). Screen European CLO pools for combined Liberty Global / Altice telecom concentration before bidding mezzanine.
9. **The European CLO 'Triple Squeeze' Filter (2026-10-29):** Loan repricings have compressed European CLO portfolio WAS to ~350 bps (~40 bps below the 10-year historical average), leaving the average WAS cushion at just ~10 bps alongside ~0.9 pt WARF and ~3.9% WARR cushions. Never underwrite European CLO junior/mezzanine notes ('F' / BB / B) inside the ~10% of low-WAS deals concurrently below <10 bps WAS, <1.0 pt WARF, and <1.0% WARR cushions — managers trapped in that triple squeeze cannot trade out of deteriorating credits without failing par/OC tests.
10. **Circular AI SPV Debt vs IG Hyperscaler `AAAsf` Data-Centre ABS & BDC Rating Bifurcation (2026-10-29):** Across the $2.9tn data-centre CapEx cycle ($1.5tn external debt gap; $30–40bn/yr ABS/CMBS), strictly separate `AAAsf` data-centre ABS backed by binding IG hyperscaler leases from Moody's-flagged 'circular financing' SPVs and PIK-toggle neocloud loans facing a 2027–2029 refinancing cliff.
